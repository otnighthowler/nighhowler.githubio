Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Hearth ("the app") is a social media scheduling tool operated by nighthowler ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles when you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, and what happens to it.

Where your data lives

Hearth runs on the computer of the person using it. Account tokens, scheduled posts and uploaded media are stored in files on that computer. We don't operate a server that collects or stores your TikTok data, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information we access from TikTok

With your permission, granted on TikTok's own login screen, Hearth accesses:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): your open ID, display name and username, so you can tell your connected accounts apart.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): to send videos you schedule to your TikTok account, either as a direct post or to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so scheduled posts can be published while you're away.

Hearth only uploads content that you have chosen and scheduled yourself. It doesn't read your messages, followers, likes or other users' data.

How we use it

  • To show which of your accounts are connected.
  • To publish the videos and captions you scheduled, at the times you chose, with the privacy and interaction settings you chose.
  • To show you the result (for example, a link to the published post or an error message).

We don't sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok (or Instagram) to carry out the posts you scheduled.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect an account at any time from Hearth's Accounts page, which deletes its tokens and unpublished posts from the app. You can also revoke Hearth's access from your TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the Hearth folder removes everything the app stored.

Security

Tokens are stored only on the local computer. The app is reachable only from that computer (localhost) and refuses requests from other websites.

Children

Hearth isn't directed at children under 13 and must not be used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
