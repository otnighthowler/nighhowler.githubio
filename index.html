Plan a week of posts across your accounts, in one place.

Hearth is a scheduling app that runs on the creator's own computer. It's used to plan, review and publish videos to the TikTok accounts its owner manages.

How Hearth uses TikTok

  1. Log in with TikTok. The creator connects each of their TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Login Kit. Hearth never sees a TikTok password.
  2. Plan the week. In the week planner, the creator picks a video, writes a caption and chooses a date, time and which of their accounts it goes to.
  3. Review before posting. Every post can be previewed and test-checked. The creator chooses who can see it (privacy level) and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed.
  4. Publish. At the scheduled time, Hearth uploads the video with the Content Posting API, either publishing directly or sending it to the creator's TikTok inbox to finish in the app.

What Hearth is not

Hearth doesn't scrape TikTok, automate likes, follows or comments, or post to accounts its user doesn't own. Each account must be connected by its owner through TikTok's own login screen, and can be disconnected at any time.
